Definition 1.19.3. Discrete log (or index).

Let \(n\) be a positive integer, let \(r\in (\Z/n\Z)^*\) be a unit of order \(\ord r=m\text{,}\) and let \(s\in \angvec{r}\text{.}\) The unique integer \(k\in \{0,1,\dots, m-1\}\) such that \(s=r^k\) is called the discrete log (or index) of \(s\) to the base \(r\text{,}\) and is denoted by \(\ind_r s\text{.}\)

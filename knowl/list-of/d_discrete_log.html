Definition 1.19.5. Discrete log (or index).

Let \(n\) be a positive integer, let \(g\in (\Z/n\Z)^*\) be a unit of order \(\ord r=m\text{,}\) and let \(h\in \angvec{g}\text{.}\) The unique integer \(k\in \{0,1,\dots, m-1\}\) such that \(h=g^k\) is called the discrete log (or index) of \(h\) to the base \(g\text{,}\) and is denoted by \(\log_g h\) (or \(\ind_g h\)).

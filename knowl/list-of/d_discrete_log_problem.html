Definition 1.19.4. Discrete log problem.

Let \(p\) be a prime integer, let \(g=[a]_p\in \F_p^*\) be a primitive root, and let \(h=[b]_p\in \F_p^*\text{.}\) Find an integer \(n\in \{0,1,\dots, p-2\}\) such that \(h=g^n\text{,}\) or equivalently, such that \(a^n\equiv b\pmod p\text{.}\) In other words, compute \(\ind_g h\text{.}\)

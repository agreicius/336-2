Definition 1.19.4. Discrete log problem.

Let \(p\) be a prime integer, let \(r=[a]_p\in \F_p^*\text{,}\) and let \(s=[b]_p\in \angvec{r}\text{.}\) Find an integer \(n\) such that \(s=r^n\text{,}\) or equivalently, such that \(a^n\equiv b\pmod p\text{.}\) In other words find an integer \(x\) solving the equation
\begin{equation*} r^x=s\text{.} \end{equation*}
